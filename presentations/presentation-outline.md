## Slide 6

A professional error caused by AI may already fall within E&O, depending on wording. The incremental promise must be identified before an additional charge. The proposed endorsement should affirm specified disclosed uses, define applicable limits, resolve defence arrangements and require material-change reporting. Existing policy wording is not supplied, so this table lists design decisions rather than claiming a verified coverage gap. Claims-made/occurrence, retroactive dates, known circumstances, related claims, counsel/settlement and reinsurance must also be resolved.

## Slide 7

A is a standalone product structure, not an obligation to insure all AI uses. Both A and B can use limits, exclusions and renewal review. B may use lower implementation and channel costs because TSI already knows SME customers, but these costs are not quantified in the case. C means active capability development, including customer discovery, shadow underwriting, incident study, controls and reinsurance negotiation. It lacks new-product insured-claims learning but has nonzero learning value. Choose B after launch gates, C if gates fail, and revisit A when distinct demand justifies separate product costs.

## Slide 8

Illustrative economics, not case observations or a quote: 100 full policy-years, CAD 5,000 annual premium, 25% variable costs, CAD 100,000 fixed costs, CAD 100,000 mean covered cost including defence. At annual claims per policy of 1%, 2%, 3%, combined ratios are 65%,85%,105% and profits CAD 175k,75k,-25k. Break-even frequency is 2.75%; a 10% underwriting margin reduces it to 2.25%. Separate reinsurance, taxes, investment return and capital charges are omitted. The source incident mean is not substituted for contractual covered cost.

## Slide 9

This is a deterministic common-vendor stress, not an expected-loss or probabilistic capital model. At CAD 250,000 covered cost per affected policy, 5,10,20 policies cost CAD 1.25M,2.5M,5M before any applicable recoveries. Track shared vendor/model versions, affected clients and policy limits. Compare stresses with approved capital/risk budgets and treaty response. Policy limits do not themselves diversify common dependencies. Distinguish deployed limits, expected loss, gross stress and net capital requirement.

## Slide 10

Months 0–3 prepare with customer demand, technical pricing and policy mapping. Months 3–6 bind selected verified uses after legal, capital, reinsurance and claims/data sign-off. Monitor control drift, notifications, defence costs and common dependencies monthly/quarterly. The earliest first annual renewal is Month 15, not Month 12 from project inception. Use it to judge continuation and future terms. Expansion requires sufficiently mature loss and reserve evidence, not a zero-claim first year. Stopping new business does not extinguish already incurred obligations.
