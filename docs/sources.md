## Public primary sources

1. [The Geneva Association, Gen AI Risks for Businesses: Exploring the Role for Insurance (2025)](https://www.genevaassociation.org/publication/digital-ai-transformation/gen-ai-risks-businesses-exploring-role-insurance-0). Figure 14 supports relative insurance demand by usage/failure categories. Its values are indexed, not respondent percentages. The survey covers China, France, Germany, Japan, the UK and US, not Canada.
2. [HSB, Introduces AI Liability Insurance for Small Businesses, March 18, 2026](https://www.munichre.com/hsb/en/press-and-publications/press-releases/2026/2026-03-18-introducing-ai-liability-insurance-for-small-businesses.html). Supports a partner-policy distribution precedent for SME AI coverage. Published protection includes certain bodily injury, property damage and advertising/IP pathways. It is not evidence of TSI profitability, identical scope or Canadian availability.
3. [Coalition, Adds New Affirmative AI Endorsement to U.S. Surplus and Canada Cyber Insurance Policies](https://www.coalitioninc.com/announcements/coalition-adds-new-affirmative-ai-endorsement-to-cyber-policies). Supports affirmative AI wording in a Cyber policy context, not an equivalent E&O liability product.
4. [Statistics Canada, Analysis on Expected Use of Artificial Intelligence by Businesses in Canada, Third Quarter of 2025](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/11-621-m/11-621-m2025011-eng.pdf). Footnote 3 explains that planned usage includes some current users. Do not add 14.5% to the historical 12.2% as distinct populations.
5. [Supreme Court of Canada, Progressive Homes Ltd. v. Lombard General Insurance Co. of Canada, 2010 SCC 33](https://decisions.scc-csc.ca/scc-csc/scc-csc/en/item/7874/index.do). Supports the possibility-of-coverage framework for a duty-to-defend analysis under the wording and pleadings. It does not settle a hypothetical AI endorsement or all defence-cost allocation questions.

## Historical research not independently reproduced

The source deck cites AIID/CSET summaries and a DAIL extract dated **Aug. 2, 2026**. That historical extraction date is preserved in the record. Without raw records, IDs, filters, missing-value rules and a versioned snapshot, this revision does not treat the reported percentages as reproduced findings. Dynamic current websites cannot reconstruct a historical extract automatically.

The original cover's speaker notes identify its artwork as OpenAI image generation created for Team Analysts in **August 2026**. The revision reuses that artwork; its illustrative people are not portraits of the named team members.
